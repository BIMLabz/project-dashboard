You grade the model for a long job. The harness dropped a recovery path. The score looks like a model fail. It was a membrane fail.

Prime Agent (Karten 2026) is a systems harness for long-horizon work: persistent IPython REPL, recursive sub-agents that stay addressable after they finish, continual refinement of prompts and skills, plus recovery and verification so harness bugs do not get blamed on the weights.

On identical weights in the paper, ARC-AGI-3 Best@1 goes 30% -> 95.5%.

Steal recovery and verification ideas. Do not treat that number as your product score. Measure the model's ceiling. Do not let a flaky harness flatten it.
