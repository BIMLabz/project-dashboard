# X posts tab: numbering rule (append-only)

The X tab (`group-x-account.html`) is a numbered list. Each entry links to its own detail page, `x-post-<id>.html`.

- Numbers are global and stable. Tab filters and search hide items but never renumber them.
- Order: covered items (published on @BuiltinMind) come first in publish order. Then every not-covered or dropped item follows in the order it was added.
- New posts are always **appended at the end** with the next number. Never insert in the middle. Never reuse a number.
- Posts are covered in number order. Newer topics come later.
- Badges:
  - **Covered**: a real public @BuiltinMind post or X Article exists. Record its date and title in the item note.
  - **Not covered yet**: drafted, held or waiting.
  - **Dropped**: Ashish dropped it. Keep the number; do not delete the entry.
- A post becomes Covered only from evidence: check the public timeline through the read-only X connector. It keeps its number when its badge changes.
- Every tweet on a detail page has a figure under it when it references one, with a one-line caption. Each tweet also has Copy buttons: text plus image, with text-only and image-only fallbacks.
